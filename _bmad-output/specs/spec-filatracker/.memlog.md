- (event) Create intent: distill finalized PRD, UX, and Architecture Spine into canonical spec kernel and adopted companions
- (direction) Rich structured input: direct extraction selected; no elicitation required
- (decision) Why: Brazilian 3D-printer users currently search specialty stores separately; FilaTracker provides trusted pt-BR comparison by taxonomy, Listing Price and R$/kg with honest merchant handoff, not frete-inclusive checkout.
- (capability) CAP-1 Offer search and discovery — intent: anonymous shoppers search and browse filament Offers across active Homologated Stores by free text, Material Family, Specific Type, and brand; success: multi-store results, family rollups with labeled child types, login-free browse, and explicit no-match state.
- (capability) CAP-2 Filtering and deterministic ranking — intent: shoppers narrow and order Offers for like-for-like comparison; success: required filters work, default diameter includes 1.75mm plus unknown, sort supports Listing Price or valid R$/kg, and availability/price/freshness ordering is deterministic.
- (capability) CAP-3 Trustworthy Offer comparison — intent: shoppers compare grouped or unmatched Offers using objective price, merchant, availability, freshness and filament attributes; success: dense comparison exposes source facts, omits fabricated R$/kg, uses no imagery/logos, and displays frete disclaimers beside ranking or cheapest claims.
- (capability) CAP-4 Safe taxonomy and Merge — intent: system groups only genuinely equivalent Offers while uncertain Offers stay independently searchable; success: exact normalized brand + Specific Type + weight is the only Merge key, color/diameter are excluded, ambiguous or incomplete Offers remain unmatched, and bundles never gain fabricated unit economics.
- (capability) CAP-5 Merchant handoff and measurement — intent: shoppers leave for the merchant through stable measurable affiliate-ready handoff; success: every Ver na loja uses the internal outbound resolver, reaches an allowed Store destination, records a countable Outbound Click without blocking navigation, and can later add disclosed affiliate parameters.
- (capability) CAP-6 Per-Offer price history — intent: shoppers inspect stored Listing Price history for one Store Offer; success: detail renders per-Offer history when available and an honest insufficient-history state otherwise, with no Merge aggregate or alert/signup UI.
- (capability) CAP-7 Governed Store coverage — intent: operators ingest and expose filament Offers only from policy-compliant Homologated Stores; success: five named launch Stores pass fixture and capacity gates, broken/blocked Stores become Unsupported, incomplete runs cannot mass-mark OOS, and public coverage excludes Unsupported Stores.
- (decision) Success signal: a shopper completes anonymous search → comparison → Ver na loja and the resolver records Outbound Click; launch success metric identity is Outbound Click count, with numeric threshold deferred until traffic baseline.
- (constraint) v1 is a public responsive web product, strongly mobile-usable, fully anonymous, pt-BR and BRL only; no native app or authentication.
- (constraint) Day-1 value is searchable Offers rather than a mature canonical catalog; launch set is Closin, Voolt3D, 3D Colors, Filamentos 3D Brasil and Topink3D, with filament-only ingestion from multi-category Stores.
- (constraint) Listing Price excludes frete and is the only ranking price; unknown weight never yields R$/kg, promotions require Store-supplied higher original price, and no price/currentness/checkout guarantee may be implied.
- (constraint) Offers unrefreshed for more than 48 hours remain visible with stale qualification; unavailable and stale active/degraded Offers remain outbound-capable.
- (constraint) Required IA is home, search results, Offer/Merge detail, Material Family browse, Brand browse and trust/legal surfaces; home is search-first and comparison stays in dense image-free tables/lists.
- (constraint) Every outbound action is labeled Ver na loja, announces external navigation and uses the internal resolver; history is per Offer URL only; core flow targets WCAG 2.1 AA with keyboard controls, visible focus, textual statuses and responsive readable tables.
- (constraint) Scraping is deterministic with versioned per-Store maps, fixture homologation and offline-only AI assistance; runtime LLM/ML matching, CAPTCHA bypass, credentialed scraping, robots disregard and production auto-repair are forbidden.
- (constraint) Blocked or broken Stores become Unsupported; incomplete/failed runs preserve prior published state and cannot infer absence. If fewer than five Stores are active, full-coverage claims pause and replacement is attempted within 14 days.
- (constraint) The finalized Architecture Spine is normative; implementation must satisfy AD-1 through AD-25, including the hexagonal modular monolith and deterministic shared ingestion pipeline.
- (constraint) D1 is the sole authoritative store; Offer/Merge/history/run lineage is durable while FTS and membership projections are rebuildable. Publication is atomic, generation-fenced, replay-safe and cannot expose mixed or partial generations.
- (constraint) Exactly two Workers exist: public web has no D1 or ingest authority and uses a typed Service Binding; non-public ingest/data alone owns D1, queues, schedules, Store access and publication.
- (constraint) Persisted, queue, RPC and presentation boundaries use canonical versioned contracts with N/N-1 rollout compatibility; dynamic pages use one bounded epoch-consistent RPC snapshot and backend failure never appears as an empty result.
- (constraint) Merchant content, destinations, telemetry and public commands are untrusted, allowlisted, redacted and resource-bounded; analytics minimizes PII and remains limited to product events.
- (constraint) Only local and production environments exist; local/CI cannot access production authority. Dynamic MVP responses have no cross-request cache.
- (constraint) Production deploy is blocked until identity, compatibility, publication, replay, security, privacy, recovery, performance and accessibility invariants pass; provisional targets are search p95 <500ms and detail LCP <2.5s.
- (decision) Non-goal: no marketplace/cart/checkout/payment, seller portal, public write or partner API, native app, authentication, accounts, alerts, watchlists, reviews, email capture, push or notifications in MVP.
- (decision) Non-goal: no frete/CEP/tax/landed-cost ranking, price guarantees, Mercado Livre aggregation, images/logos/proxies, visual catalog grids, deal rails or vitrine merchandising.
- (decision) Non-goal: no spool inventory, print-farm/printer integration, resin/accessories/non-filament products, Merge-level history, color/diameter Merge, forced/probabilistic matching or production AI/LLM behavior.
- (decision) Non-goal: no day-1 affiliate dependency, display ads, programmatic SEO farms, Store microsites, best-deal/recently-reduced experiences or policy-violating coverage optimization.
- (assumption) v1 remains pt-BR and BRL only; search/product-view events may be diagnostic but are not success metrics; no display ads.
- (assumption) Outbound Click numeric target will be set after initial public traffic; provisional performance targets and WCAG 2.1 AA stand until replaced by reviewed acceptance values.
